Our Story

Since our inception, ASS Auto Garage has been driven by a singular obsession: automotive perfection. Founded in the heart of Khanpur, Punjab, we recognized a gap in the market for truly high-end car body care that treats every vehicle as a masterpiece.

What started as a small specialist workshop has grown into the region's premier destination for luxury car painting and structural repair. Our dedication to quality is not just a promise; it's the core of our technical DNA.
